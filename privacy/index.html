Rising Racer – Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 21, 2026 · Publisher and data controller: Yiğit Oğuzhan Atmaca · Contact: atmacay.oguzhan@gmail.com · Türkçe

This policy explains what information the mobile game Rising Racer (the “Game”) collects, why, and the choices you have. The Game is published by Yiğit Oğuzhan Atmaca (“we”, “us”), who is the data controller for the processing described here. By using the Game you agree to this policy.

1. What we collect

We do not ask for your name, email address, phone number or contacts, and the Game has no account or login. Your career, garage and settings are stored on your device. Your game progress (career, profile, items and purchase records, not your settings) is also backed up to your platform's cloud: Google Play Games Saved Games on Android, linked to your Google account, or iCloud on iOS, linked to your Apple ID. This backup lets you continue on a new phone or after reinstalling; we cannot read it or link it to your identity.

The Game uses third-party services that collect the following automatically:

  • Device and app information: device model, operating system, language, app version, IP address and the approximate (country/region-level) location derived from it.
  • Advertising identifiers: Google Advertising ID (Android) or Apple IDFA (iOS, only if you allow tracking), and similar identifiers set by ad partners.
  • Gameplay and usage events: for example game opened, career started, race started or finished, rewarded ad watched, session length.
  • Purchase information: which in-app product was bought, price and currency. Payments are handled entirely by Google Play or the Apple App Store; we never see your card or payment details.
  • Crash and performance data: diagnostic information when the Game crashes or misbehaves.

2. Why we use it

  • To show ads, including rewarded video ads you choose to watch, and to measure and limit how often ads are shown.
  • To measure which of our own advertising campaigns brought players to the Game.
  • To understand how the Game is played and to fix bugs and crashes.
  • To process and restore in-app purchases.
  • To back up your game progress and restore it on a new device or after reinstalling.

Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, UK and Switzerland) we ask for your consent before personalised advertising and analytics start. Our legal bases are your consent, our legitimate interest in running and improving the Game, and the performance of a contract when you make a purchase.

4. Your choices

  • Consent and privacy choices: where a consent form is shown, you can change your choice at any time in the Game under Me → Settings → Privacy.
  • iOS tracking: you can allow or deny tracking when asked, and change it later in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking.
  • Android ads: you can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Privacy → Ads).
  • Delete my data: Me → Settings → Privacy → Delete my data erases everything the Game stores on your device and deletes the cloud backup of your progress. Uninstalling the Game removes the data on the device only; the cloud backup stays so you can restore it later. You can also remove it in your Google Play Games or iCloud settings.
  • Data held by partners: to access or delete data held by the services above, email us with your advertising ID (shown in your device settings) and we will forward your request, or contact the service directly.

5. Retention and security

On-device data stays until you delete it or uninstall the Game. The cloud backup stays until you use Delete my data or remove it in your Google Play Games or iCloud settings. Third-party services keep data for the periods described in their policies (for example Firebase Analytics keeps event data for up to 14 months). Data is sent over encrypted (HTTPS) connections. Some partners process data outside your country, including in the United States, using legally recognised safeguards.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13 (or the minimum age in your country) and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided information, contact us and we will help delete it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, delete or port your data, to object to or restrict processing, to withdraw consent and to complain to your data-protection authority. Contact us at atmacay.oguzhan@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

8. Users in Türkiye (KVKK)

For users in Türkiye, the Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 (KVKK) applies. The data controller is Yiğit Oğuzhan Atmaca. Data is collected automatically through the services in section 3 for the purposes in section 2, on the legal grounds of your explicit consent (personalised ads and analytics), the performance of a contract (purchases) and our legitimate interest (running the Game, fixing crashes). Because these providers operate servers abroad, including in the United States, data is transferred outside Türkiye with your explicit consent or the safeguards in Article 9 of the KVKK. You have the rights listed in Article 11 of the KVKK; the full notice is in the Turkish version.
